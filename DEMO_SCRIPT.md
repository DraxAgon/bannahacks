# Fear Ladder · 3-minute demo script

Setup: the live URL is open on `/`. A Gemini key is set on the deployment, and the demo spider ladder is pre-loaded in a second tab. Screen at 1440px, browser zoom 100%.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:20** | Landing hero, then scroll slowly to the "Why" stats | "About one in eleven U.S. adults has a specific phobia this year, and only about 11% get adequate treatment. Exposure therapy works, but building a good hierarchy takes time therapists don't have. Fear Ladder is for that therapist." |
| **0:20–0:50** | Click **Build a ladder** → Spiders → paste *"Big hairy spiders, worst when they move toward me; tiny ones far away are fine."* → **Generate ladder**. Rungs appear and develop from sepia to colour, one by one. | "I describe the fear in plain words. A planner spaces nine rungs evenly in SUDS and turns each one into eight visual dials. One anchor image locks the spider's identity, then each rung is generated, post-processed and checked by a vision critic. They appear as they pass." |
| **0:50–1:20** | Scroll the finished ladder from rung 1 (the word *spider* on a card) to rung 9 (close photo on an open palm); tap a couple to reveal. Open **Under the hood**: planner JSON → a rung's exact prompt and sharp params → critic JSON → the **target vs estimated** chart. | "Same spider on every rung, from the word to a close photo. Everything stays blurred until someone chooses to look. Under the hood you see the planner's JSON, each rung's exact prompt, the deterministic blur and saturation, every critic score, and how close the estimated intensity lands to the target." |
| **1:20–2:15** | Switch to the demo ladder tab → **Start session** → Begin. Predict "I'll panic", 70% likely. **Hold** to look (release once to show the blur), rate SUDS falling with the arrow keys. Done → "Did it happen?" **No**, surprise high, "It stayed still." Climb once. On the next rung, rate peak 80 and end 60 → **Add a half-step** → "Drawing a smaller step" → the new rung slides into the ladder. | "Before looking, the patient names the feared outcome and how likely it is. They control the reveal: let go and it blurs instantly, and every release is counted. Their prediction didn't happen, so the app suggests climbing. Here they get stuck, so the app draws a half-step between the two rungs, live, checked for safety, and slides it in." |
| **2:15–2:40** | **Open the report**: SUDS curves, peak vs end, expectancy, then the **fear fingerprint** radar drawing itself. | "The therapist gets a report: distress over time, expectancy before and after, looking time and releases, and the patient's own words. The fear fingerprint is a small ridge regression trained live on this patient's ratings. It says proximity drives their fear more than realism, and it's labelled exploratory." |
| **2:40–3:00** | Footer: safety line, SynthID note, Delete all local data. | "It's for specific phobias only, with content rules enforced three times and nothing that auto-advances. There are no accounts; everything stays in the browser. Next: a clinician pilot, more phobias, and AR at the top of the ladder." |

**Backup plan:** if live generation is slow or rate-limited, use the demo ladder for the whole demo (identical screens), and show a pre-recorded live build for 0:20–0:50.
